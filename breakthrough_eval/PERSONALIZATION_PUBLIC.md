# Public personalization

No PersonaMem, PersonaMem-v2, LaMP, or PersonaAgent result has been reproduced locally. Those
datasets/harnesses are absent, and literature values in `PRIOR_ART_2026.md` are not mixed with local
results.

The frozen perspective gauntlet passes its synthetic role/authority cases, and the 250-scenario
suite correctly filters wrong-role, wrong-tenant, and authority-mismatch evidence. These are policy
unit/integration probes, not evidence of evolving realistic preference modeling.

The required public comparison remains: no profile, profile text, full history, RAG history,
structured dictionary, summary profile, dense memory, agentic profile memory, StrongStructuredBaseline,
and HNG under identical reader/task budgets. Structured exact profile state must be separated from
semantic fuzzy perspective. Until official update, uncertainty, and implicit-preference metrics are
run, dynamic personalization is `NOT_RUN` and no advantage is claimed.
